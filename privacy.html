Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rangers Guild ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile apps and games (the "Apps"). By using the Apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.

Some game data, such as progress, scores and settings, may be stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our Apps may display ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic and crash data related to ad delivery

This information is processed by Google according to its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Personalized ads and your choices

  • Consent: Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings in the App, if available.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • iOS: You can control app tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

4. How information is used

Information collected through third-party services is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve the stability and quality of the Apps. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Third-party services

The Apps may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

6. Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our Apps, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Data security and retention

We do not store personal information on our own servers. Data stored locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not directly hold personal information, most requests can be handled through the settings above or through the relevant third-party provider. You can also contact us for help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via github.com/rangers-guild.
